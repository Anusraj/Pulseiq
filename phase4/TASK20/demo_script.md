# Five-minute demo script

## Demo link

response. The API deployment instructions are documented in

## Timed runbook

**0:00–0:30 — Context**  
“PulseIQ turns validated HR and operating feeds into governed metrics and
decision views. I will show growth, cash, delivery, and the trust gate.”

**0:30–1:30 — Workforce**  
Open the dashboard. Show the reporting-period control and headcount trend.
Point out the 392-to-440 increase and explain that suppressed small groups do
not appear.

**1:30–2:20 — Movement and retention**  
Open the Attrition page or KPI Explorer. Show the latest attrition value and
explain why the rolling view is safer for board discussion than a single
month.

**2:20–3:10 — Cash and cost**  
Open Profit & Loss and Receivables & Cash. Show DSO at 48 days and the largest
overdue balance. State clearly that the June payroll-to-ledger mismatch blocks
a cost intervention.

**3:10–4:00 — Teams and privacy**  
Open KPI Explorer. Select `manager.demo`, show that only delivery metrics and
Team drill-down are available, then explain that small groups are hidden.

**4:00–5:00 — Handover**  
Show the board deck's final slide and the DQ caveat. Close with: “Use PulseIQ
now for monitoring people, cash, and delivery; return after Finance closes
DQ-C01 before making a payroll-cost decision.”
